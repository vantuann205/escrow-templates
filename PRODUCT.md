# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Nervos Spark reviewers evaluating whether the prototype addresses a real CKB holder need, and CKB holders considering their first Nervos DAO deposit.

## Product Purpose

DAO Shelter Check helps holders model an illustrative secondary-issuance compensation scenario before depositing CKB. It makes the assumptions, occupied capacity, and two-phase withdrawal process visible so users can judge the trade-offs. For Spark reviewers, the prototype demonstrates a concrete holder workflow rather than claiming adoption or live protocol connectivity.

## Brand Personality

Precise, approachable, kinetic. The experience should feel credible to protocol-aware users while remaining legible to a first-time DAO depositor.

## Anti-references

- A pixel-for-pixel copy of AdsCrawl or reuse of its brand assets and source.
- Crypto interfaces that imply live network data, guaranteed yield, price protection, or a wallet connection that does not exist.
- Decorative blockchain jargon, speculative metrics, or a generic token-trading dashboard.

## Design Principles

- Put the holder's estimate and the CKB/DAO model at the center of the experience.
- Distinguish protocol facts from editable demo assumptions.
- Explain the lock and exit path before encouraging a first deposit.
- Use motion to make the modeled globe understandable, never to imply live chain activity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences. Essential calculations and protocol caveats must remain available without WebGL or animation.
